# ADR-019 Music/SFX catalogue: buy-out + CC0 + reviewed AI audio before partner APIs

**Decision.** Every catalogue asset carries a `license_record` (type, licensor, SaaS sublicensing, end-customer commercial use, attribution, territory, expiry, evidence). Start with commissioned buy-out tracks and individually verified CC0 sources; AI-generated audio only after terms review; partner APIs (Epidemic, Artlist, Soundstripe, Uppbeat) when their SaaS terms are verified. Expired/restricted assets are excluded from new renders. (Phase 4; terms still unverified.)
